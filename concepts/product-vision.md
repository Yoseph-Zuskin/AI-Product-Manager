# Product Vision

The **AI Strategies for Business Transformations** program treats vision as thefirst pillar ofcorporate AI strategy: a comprehensive plan for harnessing AI to drive businessoutcomes rests ona clear picture of what the AI-powered — eventually autonomous — enterpriselooks like three tofive years out, the business outcomes it wants to achieve, and where to focusresources.[^p1-product-vision][^r-business-department]
That vision then cascades through initiatives (use cases and data), technology
(platforms andinfrastructure), and people (talent, organization, and governance), eachanswering three guidingquestions. Where-to-play choices follow from sources of advantage, above all therichness ofproprietary data mapped across the customer, operations, administrative, andrisk quadrants of theAI Radar 2.0.[^p1-product-vision][^r-software-system] This requires viewing theenterprise as a value chain — frontoffice (customers), operations, and support functions — so the vision isgrounded in theprocesses AI will actually enhance.[^p1-product-vision][^r-business-department]
The **AI-Driven Product Strategy** program applies the same logic at the productlevel. Productvision is the North Star: an articulation of the end state, who the product orcompany aims tobecome, and the value delivered to users and stakeholders, aligned by contrastwith the productmission, which describes the journey of what the team does today to reach thatdestination.[^p2-product-vision][^r-persona]
A good vision answers three questions — the future to create, the problem beingsolved and why itmatters, and who benefits and how their lives change — and passes six tests: itis ambitious
(enough to seem impossible in the short run), inspiring, unique, enduring,
vivid, and easy toexplain.[^p2-product-vision][^r-persona] Strategy then translates customerproblems into products that aredesirable, feasible, and viable, cast into execution via frameworks such asV2MOM and the ProductStrategy Canvas, whose layers run from vision, mission, and values down througharenas, bets, andcompetencies to metrics andhurdles.[^p2-product-vision][^r-software-system][^r-business-department]
The two programs converge on vision as the alignment device that anchors alldownstream
decision-making and prioritization. The C-suite's "AI-powered enterprise" is theenterprise-levelrendition of the product manager's North Star, and both insist the vision stayproblem- ratherthan solution-focused: the programs warn against product myopia — falling inlove with the productand losing sight of the customer problem — and reframe the product manager as aproblem manager,
CEО-of-the-product, customer advocate, and bridge builder acrossfunctions.[^p2-product-vision][^r-persona][^r-machine-learning-algorithm][^r-software-system]
The practical takeaway in the AI era is that vision does double duty. Itdescribes the end stateof products that are increasingly intelligent, adaptive, and personalized, andit guides how theproduct manager works: shifting from producing analysis and artifacts toorchestrating outcomes,
and building emerging competencies such as prompt engineering, model literacy,
agent design, dataproduct thinking, and ethicaloversight.[^p2-product-vision][^r-persona][^r-machine-learning-algorithm][^r-software-system]
Professionally, a productvision
should be stated on one page, be aspirational yet concrete, and always tracefrom the future stateback to the problem that makes it matter — whether that future state is aproduct or the wholeenterprise.
