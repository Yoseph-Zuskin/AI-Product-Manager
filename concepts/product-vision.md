# Product Vision

The **AI Strategies for Business Transformations** program treats vision as the first pillar of corporate AI strategy: a comprehensive plan for harnessing AI to drive business outcomes rests ona clear picture of what the AI-powered — eventually autonomous — enterpriselooks like three to five years out, the business outcomes it wants to achieve, and where to focusresources.[^p1-product-vision][^r-business-department]
That vision then cascades through initiatives (use cases and data), technology
(platforms and infrastructure), and people (talent, organization, and governance), eachanswering three guiding questions. Where-to-play choices follow from sources of advantage, above all the richness of proprietary data mapped across the customer, operations, administrative, and risk quadrants of theAI Radar 2.0.[^p1-product-vision][^r-software-system] This requires viewing the enterprise as a value chain — frontoffice (customers), operations, and support functions — so the vision is grounded in the processes AI will actually enhance.[^p1-product-vision][^r-business-department]
The **AI-Driven Product Strategy** program applies the same logic at the productlevel. Productvision is the North Star: an articulation of the end state, who the product or company aims to become, and the value delivered to users and stakeholders, aligned by contrastwith the productmission, which describes the journey of what the team does today to reach that destination.[^p2-product-vision][^r-persona]
A good vision answers three questions — the future to create, the problem being solved and why it matters, and who benefits and how their lives change — and passes six tests: itis ambitious
(enough to seem impossible in the short run), inspiring, unique, enduring,
vivid, and easy to explain.[^p2-product-vision][^r-persona] Strategy then translates customer problems into products that are desirable, feasible, and viable, cast into execution via frameworks such asV2MOM and the Product Strategy Canvas, whose layers run from vision, mission, and values down througharenas, bets, and competencies to metrics and hurdles.[^p2-product-vision][^r-software-system][^r-business-department]
The two programs converge on vision as the alignment device that anchors alldownstream
decision-making and prioritization. The C-suite's "AI-powered enterprise" is the enterprise-levelrendition of the product manager's North Star, and both insist the vision stayproblem- ratherthan solution-focused: the programs warn against product myopia — falling in love with the product and losing sight of the customer problem — and reframe the product manager as aproblem manager,
CEO-of-the-product, customer advocate, and bridge builder across functions.[^p2-product-vision][^r-persona][^r-machine-learning-algorithm][^r-software-system]
The practical takeaway in the AI era is that vision does double duty. Itdescribes the end stateof products that are increasingly intelligent, adaptive, and personalized, and it guides how the product manager works: shifting from producing analysis and artifacts to orchestrating outcomes,
and building emerging competencies such as prompt engineering, model literacy,
agent design, data product thinking, and ethicaloversight.[^p2-product-vision][^r-persona][^r-machine-learning-algorithm][^r-software-system]
Professionally, a product vision
should be stated on one page, be aspirational yet concrete, and always trace from the future state back to the problem that makes it matter — whether that future state is a product or the whole enterprise.

## Definition

A framework for defining and communicating compelling product vision. Covers north star articulation, V2MOM framework, strategy canvas, and AI-era vision that anchors teams on problem-focused north star. Includes vision articulation templates and stakeholder alignment techniques.
