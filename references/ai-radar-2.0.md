# AI Radar 2.0

Enterprise-wide framework for mapping Gen AI opportunities and surfacing initiatives with the greatest business impact, as referenced in the Kellogg AI Strategies for Business Transformation program.

## Framework Components

AI Radar 2.0 maps opportunities across enterprise dimensions, typically including:

- **Technology tools** — Gen AI capabilities and model options
- **Data** — Proprietary data assets and governance
- **Talent & expertise** — Team skills and capacity
- **Execution capabilities** — Change management and operational readiness
- **Problem definition** — Clear articulation of business problems worth solving

## Key Principles

- Maps initiatives against **business impact** and **feasibility** dimensions
- Helps surface high-potential use cases that might be overlooked
- Complements the AI Canvas 2.0 (defines *what* and *how*) with enterprise-scale opportunity assessment
- Used in the AI Strategies program to assess AI readiness and prioritize initiatives
