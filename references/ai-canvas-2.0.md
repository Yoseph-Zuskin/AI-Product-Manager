# AI Canvas 2.0 Framework

Strategic framework for planning AI initiatives developed by Kellogg School of Management (Northwestern University). Consists of three phases across nine cells:

## Three Phases

| Phase | Core Focus | Blueprint Cells |
|-------|------------|-----------------|
| **Define (Find it)** | Understanding the problem, use case, and business value | Business & customer problem, Jobs to be Done (JTBD), Business impact |
| **Design (Bottle it)** | Developing MVP/POC | Data & model management, Context & fine-tuning, MVP & rapid prototyping |
| **Deploy (Scale it)** | Productionizing, scaling, managing at industrial level | Testing & scaling, Implementation & change management, Governance & risk mitigation |

## Narrative Format (10 Questions)

1. The business problem to be addressed
2. Why the problem is important
3. How an AI-based solution will address the problem
4. Key benefits to business and customers
5. Quick financial impact estimate
6. Cross-functional team composition
7. Necessary resources (headcount, budget)
8. 90-day proof-of-concept plan
8. Hurdles/risks
9. Plan to mitigate risks
