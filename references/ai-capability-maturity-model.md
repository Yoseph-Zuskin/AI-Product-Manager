# AI Capability Maturity Model (CMM)

Organizational capability maturity framework assessing AI development across five pillars, as taught in the Kellogg AI Strategies for Business Transformation program.

## Five Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Strategy & Leadership** | Leadership commitment, vision, and governance as gating conditions for all AI initiatives |
| **Data & Infrastructure** | Data quality, accessibility, and technical infrastructure for AI development |
| **Solution Development & Deployment** | Model development, prototyping, deployment, and scaling processes |
| **Talent & Expertise** | AI skills, hiring, training, and cross-functional expertise |
| **Governance & Ethics** | Responsible AI practices, bias mitigation, compliance |

## Maturity Levels

- **Emerging** — Initial capabilities, ad-hoc processes
- **Parity** — Capabilities matching industry peers
- **Leading** — Advanced capabilities providing competitive advantage

## Practical Application

- Used to diagnose organizational AI readiness and prescribe next steps
- Scores across pillars reveal strengths and weaknesses
- The AI Capability Maturity Model is often paired with the AI Canvas 2.0 for end-to-end AI initiative planning
- Illustrated in program using AbbVie assessment case study where data and talent gaps limit strategy
