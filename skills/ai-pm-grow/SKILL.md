## Workflow

### 1. PLG Motion Selection

Choose the right growth motion for your AI product:

- **Product-Led Growth**: Self-serve, low-touch, DIY, simple to buy/adopt,
  modest price. The product itself leads: end-to-end UX so intuitive,
  frictionless, valuable that users sign up, onboard, expand on their own.

- **Sales-Led Growth**: High-touch, consultative, enterprise, complex pricing.
  Sales team leads: discovery, demo, negotiation, contract.

- **Hybrid (PLG + Sales)**: PLG for acquisition/activation, sales for expansion.
  Self-serve entry → PQL → sales-assisted expansion.

### 2. PLG Best Practices

1. **Democratization**: Messaging as empowerment — citizen designers/coders/data
   scientists (Canva, Shopify, HubSpot, Stripe)

2. **Fast Time-to-Value**: Templates let novices start from proven patterns

3. **Product Design Choices**: Frictionless onboarding, behavioral triggers,
   self-reinforcing growth loops

4. **PLG Caps**: As complexity grows, add sales assistance (PQLs → inside sales)
   rather than abandoning PLG

### 3. AI as PLG Force Multiplier

- **Pre-sale**: Generative AI → continuous insight (personas, segments,
  conversational research)

- **Onboarding**: Personalized generative onboarding coaches users to first
  value

- **Retention**: Predictive churn analysis, real-time issue resolution,
  proactive outreach

- **Expansion**: AI-driven upsell/cross-sell recommendations, usage-based
  pricing optimization

### 4. PLG Metrics

Track the right metrics for your PLG motion:

- **Activation Rate**: % users reaching first value moment
- **Time-to-Value**: Time from signup to first value realization
- **Expansion Revenue**: Net revenue retention, upsell/cross-sell rate
- **Virality**: K-factor, referral rate, invitation acceptance
- **Retention**: D1/D7/D30 retention, cohort analysis
