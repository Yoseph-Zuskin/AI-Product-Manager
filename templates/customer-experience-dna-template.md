# Customer Experience DNA (CxDNA) Framework Template

Use this template to map customer jobs across the lifecycle and pair them with organizational matching jobs, as taught in the AI Strategies for Business Transformations program.

## CxDNA Framework Overview

The CxDNA maps **Customer Action** sequences against **Organization Action** sequences across the customer lifecycle.

## Six Lifecycle Stages

| Stage | Customer Action (Jobs) | Organization Matching Jobs | AI Use Cases | Metrics |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Discover** | Researching, exploring options | Reaching, acquiring | AI insights, segmentation, targeting | Reach rate, CAC |
| **2. Learn** | Evaluating, comparing | Converting, selling more | Personalization, recommendations | Conversion rate, time-to-learn |
| **3. Evaluate** | Assessing options | Engaging | Dynamic pricing, configurators | Engagement rate, NPS |
| **4. Buy** | Purchasing, onboarding | Retaining | Fraud detection, smart contracts | Conversion rate, time-to-value |
| **5. Use** | Using product/service | Serving | Predictive support, self-service | Adoption, time-to-value |
| **6. Advocate** | Referring, reviewing | Advocating, expanding | Advocacy programs, referral AI | Referral rate, LTV |

### Service & Retention Maturity Model

| Lifecycle Stage | Crawl | Walk | Run | Fly |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | Basic segmentation | Predictive segmentation | Real-time personalization | Autonomous discovery |
| Learn | Static content | Adaptive content | AI-guided learning paths | Autonomous curriculum |
| Evaluate | Static pricing | Dynamic pricing | AI negotiation agent | Autonomous deal-making |
| Buy | Manual processing | Assisted buying | Automated fulfillment | Autonomous commerce |
| Use | Reactive support | Predictive support | Proactive optimization | Autonomous operation |
| Advocate | Loyalty programs | Referral programs | AI-driven advocacy | Autonomous advocacy |

## Three-Step Drill (Per Stage)

For each lifecycle stage:

1. **Define the stage/process** — What is the customer doing? What is the org doing?

2. **State the job to be done** — "As a [persona], when [situation], I want to [goal] so I can [outcome]"

3. **Attach AI use cases** — Map specific AI capabilities to the job
