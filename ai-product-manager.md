This concept is the top node of the AI-Product-Manager bundle. It is an **agent**: a
software-agent persona equipped with curated product-management knowledge drawn from two
Kellogg Executive Education certificate programs (Sawhney's curricula) combined with personal
concepts added as this bundle grows.

## What it is

A working set of synthesized concepts (in `concepts/`), shared templates (in `references/`),
and, when added, the user's own concepts. Each positional chain absorbs the two programs'
teachings and leaves an `## Extensions` hook for new material.
